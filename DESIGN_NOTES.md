# Esgrow Studio — design direction

The supplied Lumior image establishes a warm ivory canvas, rounded forest-green containers, citrus accents, oversized approachable type, botanical storytelling, bento cards and a monumental footer wordmark. References are visual guidance, not assets to copy or claims to reuse.

The implementation translates this into a digital garden: dimensional original SVG leaves, a floating growth chart, architectural project illustrations and organic shapes. No externally hotlinked photography. The hero balances an editorial message with an immersive garden composition. Keep headings confident and copy direct. Display the requested full value proposition beneath a shorter visual headline.

The two supplied video references add soft atmospheric greens, product-style floating UI cards, generous editorial spacing and quiet transitions. The later image set develops this into ribbed green sculpture, optical glass, mint/blue soft gradients and an immersive meadow footer with an inset ivory panel. Five supplied abstract images are now used locally across the process, services, about, partnership and journal sections. Five original generated images replace the initial illustrative hero and project placeholders: glass sprout, meadow landscape, botanical lifestyle, architectural interior and wellness still life. See `IMAGE_ASSETS.md` for sources, saved paths and final prompts. Other brands' screenshot text/logos are treated as reference material rather than copied into the site.

Motion: short sprout intro, line reveal, GSAP section entrances and scrubbed framework progress, Lenis scrolling, quiet floating artwork, project hover transformations and accessible route transitions. Disable custom motion on reduced-motion devices. Navigation and forms remain usable without animation.

Trust: examples are explicitly labelled as concept work and illustrative perspectives. Do not invent clients, ratings or performance results. Prices are indicative. Contact details and business identity must be verified before public launch.
